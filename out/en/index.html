The Hotel

Pernambuco-style hospitality

Barefoot, soulful and local.

Reclaimed wood, coconut thatch, handmade Tracunhaém ceramics and raw linen. Every detail at Corais was designed to slow time down and bring you closer to the sea.

Only 18 suites, an open-air spa and a restaurant celebrating the catch of the day — because here, luxury means space, silence and care.

Wake up to the sound of the tide and fall asleep under Pernambuco’s starry sky.
